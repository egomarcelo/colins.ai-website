What is ISO 27001?

ISO 27001 provides a framework for managing information security. In molecular design, that means protecting the information behind your research: target structures, starting compounds, experimental data, and the new molecules we design together. We apply its principles throughout the project, from the first files you share to the final candidate package.

How We Protect Your IP

The intellectual property developed for your project belongs to you. We use our proprietary AI models and workflows to carry out the design work, while keeping your project data separate from other clients' work.

  • Project Separation: Your target files, molecular designs, and simulation results stay within a dedicated project environment.
  • Confidential Inputs and Outputs: We treat both the research you provide and the candidates we generate as confidential project information.

Secure Storage & Data Transfer

  • Protected Transfers: We use encrypted connections and private cloud transfers to exchange project files with your team, including structures, candidate lists, and reports.
  • Protected Storage: Project data is encrypted at rest. The same care applies to the working files produced during docking, molecular dynamics, and binding energy calculations.

Access for the People Who Need It

  • Limited Access: Access to your data is restricted to the people assigned to your project and the tasks they need to carry out.
  • Account Protection: Multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection to the systems used for your design and simulation work.

Security Throughout the Workflow

  • Checked Tools and Inputs: We review the software used in our workflows for known security issues and validate incoming molecular files before processing them.
  • Careful Handover: Candidate structures, simulation data, and final reports are shared through the same protected channels used during the project, so your team can take the next step without exposing its research.
