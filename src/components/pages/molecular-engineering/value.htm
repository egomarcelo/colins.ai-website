Engineering

Beyond Standard Tools

At every step, we implement custom technology and workflows to ensure your binders work in the real world.

Surface & Docking

We run a detailed protein surface analysis to identify hotspots and then run custom docking workflows in order to identify favorable protein-protein interfaces (Glues/Ligands) or attachment sites (Linkers).

AI Models

For the base design process, we use customized and improved Graph and Diffusion AI models run in a local environment. This gives us a base molecular design to work with.

Electrostatic Chemistry

We manually analyze and optimize hydrogen bonds, π-stacking, hydrophobic packing, and iterate until we get sufficiently favorable energy. We have engineered ternary complexes showing sub 140 kcal/mol in MM/GBSA.

Molecular Dynamics

We run extensive MD, FEP, DFT, and MM-GBSA simulations as needed. We also modify and customize the source code of these systems depending on the goal we are trying to achieve.

Deliverables

Everything you need

We provide a complete package that allows you to start synthesis immediately.

Optimized Candidates

We understand that an energetically favorable binder does not automatically lead to degradation. We deliver 20-50 highly optimized candidates in order to maximize the probability of success in the cell lines.

Decomposition Data

We deliver a complete decomposition report that shows you the specific bonds and energies of each residue in the complex. The report includes bonding and energy data at each time frame and a full ADMET profile (TPSA, LogP).

AI Model & Source Code

We may also build and deliver a fully customized AI model, MD workflow, or additional technology for you to implement in-house. This is project based and comes at an additional cost.
